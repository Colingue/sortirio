## Context

What problem or situation forces a decision? Constraints, alternatives considered,
what is at stake.

## Decision

What was chosen, and why. Written in the present tense, affirmatively
("We use X", not "We could use X").

## Consequences

What this decision implies: what becomes mandatory, what becomes forbidden, what
cost or debt we accept.

## Usage

applies concretely.
